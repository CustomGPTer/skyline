Release notes

What changed in each version of Skyline. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.2 22 November 2026Latest

  • Check for updates now opens the new version in your browser instead of installing it from inside the app, so Skyline no longer asks for permission to install apps.
  • The small heading and intro text on the welcome screen are easier to read over a bright sky.

Version 1.0.1 22 November 2026

  • First public release. Current conditions and the next 24 hours, with a chart you can touch and a clearly labelled view of any hour.
  • A 14-day outlook that opens to 16 days, and a detailed hour-by-hour view of any day for temperature, rain, wind, visibility, humidity or pressure.
  • Rain chance and amount, wind and gust direction, UV, air quality, European pollen in season, humidity and dew point, visibility, pressure, sunrise and sunset.
  • Animated skies drawn on the phone: sunshine, cloud, rain, snow, fog, the night sky and the moon, with still, reduced and battery-saving modes.
  • Up to 20 saved places anywhere in the world, with comparison cards, reordering and removal.
  • Activity windows for walking, cycling, gardening and drying washing, and an optional site mode with your own limits and working hours.
  • Optional recent rain radar with zoom, pan and playback, and optional Met Office UK warning summaries linked to the full warnings.
  • Optional morning summary and alerts for rain, gusts, frost, heat and pollen, all starting switched off.
  • Current, Hourly and Daily widgets, each with its own place.
  • Dark, light, AMOLED black and follow-system themes, four accent colours, larger text, and cards you can reorder or hide.
  • Saved forecasts for offline use, settings export, Delete all app data, Check for updates and More apps.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.2 is called Skyline.apk. It is 105 KB (107,923 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

b6609922ea642d04182ca270f4c59a05954acbac847be2811100faa772cd35d1

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Skyline.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
