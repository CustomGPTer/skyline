What Skyline does not do

Skyline is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and update it from inside the app. Its radar shows recent rain only, not a forecast of where rain is going, and it does not push official warnings as emergency alerts.

Where the others do more

The Met Office app comes from the UK's national weather service itself, with its own forecasters, maps and warnings map. BBC Weather adds text-to-speech and personalised warnings. AccuWeather looks 45 days ahead and has storm tracking. All three are on Google Play.

Pollen and air quality

Skyline's pollen covers Europe in season, and air quality is a modelled forecast for the area, not a reading from a sensor.
